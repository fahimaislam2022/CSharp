# BuyNix Supermarket Management System

## Overview
The BuyNix Supermarket Management System is a software application designed to streamline supermarket operations. It helps supermarket managers and staff effectively manage inventory, sales, and customer interactions, leading to improved efficiency and customer satisfaction.

## Features
- **Inventory Management**: Keep track of stock levels, product details, and supplier information.
- **Sales Tracking**: Record sales transactions and generate sales reports.
- **Customer Management**: Manage customer information, including contact details and purchase history.
- **User Roles**: Define different user roles with specific permissions (e.g., admin, cashier, staff).
- **Reporting**: Generate various reports for sales, inventory, and customer activity.
- **Notifications**: Alerts for low stock levels and other important updates.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: An intuitive design that simplifies navigation and operation.
